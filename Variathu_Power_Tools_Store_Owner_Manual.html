Chapter 1 Platform Overview & System Architecture
How your custom digital showroom connects to your physical store in Kozhencherry

Welcome to your custom-built digital retail platform for Variathu Power Tools. Unlike generic third-party marketplace platforms that charge 10%–20% commission per sale, this application is 100% owned by your store. You retain direct customer relationships, zero commission fees, complete pricing autonomy, and automatic Kerala GST compliance.

🏬 Physical-to-Digital Store Synergy

Built specifically for your store at Poyanil Building, Kozhencherry. Supports both Store Counter Pickup (with secure pickup OTP codes) and Kerala Express Courier Shipping to all 14 districts in Kerala.

Customers can pay instantly using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, QR code scanning, Debit/Credit cards, or choose Cash on Delivery / Counter Payment upon collection.

3.3 Complete Order Fulfillment Workflow (Step-by-Step)

When an order arrives, follow this standard operating procedure (SOP):

A

Review Incoming Order Details in Orders Tab (?tab=orders)

New orders appear at the top with a yellow badge Order Placed. Click on the order to inspect the items, customer phone number, delivery address, and payment method (COD vs Paid Online).

B

Fulfill Courier Shipments (DTDC / TPC / APS Cargo)

If the customer selected Courier Express:

  1. Pack the power tools securely with warranty card and power accessories.
  2. Click the blue "🚚 Dispatch via Courier" button on the order card.
  3. A modal opens. Select the courier partner:
    • DTDC Express (Red & Blue Surface Express)
    • The Professional Couriers (TPC)
    • Alleppey Parcel Service (APS Cargo) (For heavy machinery like 16kg Breakers)
  4. Enter the tracking number (AWB) or click "Generate AWB".
  5. Click "Confirm & Notify Customer".
Automatic Trigger: The customer receives an immediate WhatsApp and Email stating: "Your tools are on the way with DTDC (AWB: D58291042). Track live here."
C

Fulfill Store Counter Pickups (Poyanil Building, Kozhencherry)

If the customer selected Store Counter Pickup:

  1. Pull the machinery from your showroom shelf or stock room.
  2. Click "Mark Ready for Pickup". The customer is automatically alerted via WhatsApp with pickup hours and directions to Poyanil Building.
  3. When the customer arrives at the counter, ask for their 4-digit Pickup OTP (visible on their phone screen or SMS).
  4. Verify the OTP, hand over the tools, and click "Mark Completed".
D

Marking Orders as Completed & Kerala GST Invoicing

Once delivered by the courier or collected at the counter, change status to Completed. Click the "🖨️ Print GST Invoice" button to print a physical tax invoice copy for your tax file or accounting register.

3.4 Managing Customer Cancellation Requests (?tab=cancellations)

When a customer asks to cancel an order, it appears in your dedicated Cancellations Tab with the customer’s stated reason in a prominent yellow box.

Option A: Approve Cancellation

Use if the equipment has not yet left your store.

  • Order status updates to Cancelled.
  • Machinery stock is automatically restored back into inventory.
  • Online payments trigger an automatic refund breakdown notification.
  • Customer receives an automated cancellation confirmation email.

Option B: Reject Request

Use if the shipment is already handed to the courier and cannot be recalled.

  • You enter a clear rejection explanation (e.g. "Parcel already in transit with DTDC AWB D58291042").
  • Order remains active in transit.
  • Customer is notified with instructions on how to return upon delivery.

Chapter 5 Kerala GST Compliance & Razorpay Settlements
Accounting standards, tax compliance, and automated bank settlements

5.1 Statutory Kerala GST 18% Tax Structure

Under the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) and Kerala State Goods and Services Tax (SGST) acts, standard power tools and machinery fall under the 18% GST Bracket. Your platform automatically calculates and separates these figures:

Tax Component Rate Applicability Example on ₹11,800 Tool
Taxable Value (Base) 84.75% Net Machinery Selling Price ₹10,000.00
CGST (Central Tax) 9.00% Central Government Share ₹900.00
SGST (Kerala State Tax) 9.00% Kerala State Government Share ₹900.00
Total Customer Invoice 18.00% Total Tax Final Amount Paid by Buyer ₹11,800.00

5.2 Razorpay Bank Settlement Schedule

When a customer pays online via UPI (GPay/PhonePe) or card:

  • Instant Capture: Payment is immediately verified; the order is confirmed without counter delay.
  • Direct Bank Payout (T+1 / T+2): Razorpay automatically transfers accumulated funds directly into your shop's Current Bank Account according to standard Reserve Bank of India (RBI) merchant settlement rules.
  • 0% Merchant Risk: Fraudulent or failed bank transactions are intercepted immediately; orders are only placed if payment is successfully captured.

6.2 Frequently Asked Questions (Store Owner FAQs)

Q: What if a customer says they didn't receive their pickup OTP?

A: Open the order in the Admin Orders Tab. Counter staff can verify the customer by matching their mobile number and Government ID (Aadhaar / Driving License). Once verified, you can mark the order as Completed manually.

Q: Can we change prices during special festival promotions?

A: Yes! Go to the Inventory Tab, search for the item, click the pencil icon, enter the new promotional price, and click "Update Product". It updates instantly. You can also create a promo coupon (e.g. ONAM10) in the Coupons Tab instead of changing product base prices.

Q: How do we handle heavy items like 16kg Demolition Breakers that standard couriers won't carry?

A: When clicking "Dispatch via Courier", select Alleppey Parcel Service (APS Cargo) from the dropdown. APS Cargo specializes in heavy freight and machinery across all Kerala towns.
